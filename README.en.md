Download videos and audio at the best available quality, straight to your Mac.

BGDD pairs a Chrome extension with a local engine to download content from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X, Vimeo and other supported sites.

Choose from several video and audio formats in a simple interface, with all processing handled on your Mac. Open a post in Chrome, choose a format and a destination folder, then find the saved file on your Mac, ready to use.

**Free, with no subscription or telemetry.** No BGDD account is required. BGDD is proprietary software.

## Install

1. Download and open the DMG for your Mac.
2. Launch **Installer BGDD** and follow the setup assistant.
3. Add the extension to Chrome as directed by the assistant.

Installation requires Google Chrome and an Internet connection. BGDD is not notarized by Apple: follow the [macOS authorization guide](https://github.com/enrimerveille/BGDD/blob/main/docs/DISTRIBUTION-GATEKEEPER.md) if macOS blocks it from opening.
